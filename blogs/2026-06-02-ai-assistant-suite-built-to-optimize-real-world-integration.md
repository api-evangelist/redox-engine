The AI Assistant Suite adds three AI-powered tools directly to the Redox dashboard: a Config Modifier Assistant that generates ready-to-use schemas from plain-language requests, a Log Summary Assistant that explains what happened during processing in plain English, and a Payload Summary Assistant that instantly translates raw payloads into human-readable summaries. These tools are built for mixed-technical teams—reducing reliance on support queues and freeing up technical staff for complex work. Available now for all Redox customers.
